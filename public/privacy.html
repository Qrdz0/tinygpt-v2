Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

tinygpt v2 is designed to run locally. Your account data and chats are stored in a SQLite database on this machine, and AI inference runs through Ollama on this machine. Nothing is uploaded to third-party servers by the application itself.

1. Information We Collect

DataPurposeStored
UsernameIdentify your accountLocal SQLite
PasswordAuthenticate you (stored only as a bcrypt hash)Local SQLite
Email address (optional)Send password-reset emailsLocal SQLite
Chat messagesPersist your conversationsLocal SQLite
Login dates & streakShow your usage calendarLocal SQLite
Password reset tokensSecure account recovery (expire after 1 hour)Local SQLite

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide account login, logout, and session management;
  • To store and display your chat history;
  • To send transactional emails (currently only password resets);
  • To maintain your login streak and calendar.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information;
  • We do not use your chats for advertising or model training;
  • We do not send analytics or telemetry;
  • We do not require more personal information than needed to run the app.

4. AI Processing

Your prompts are sent to a locally running Ollama instance to generate responses. On a default setup, prompt data never leaves your machine. If you reconfigure the app to point at a remote model endpoint, that endpoint's privacy practices apply to prompt data.

5. Email

Password-reset emails are sent through an SMTP provider. In development mode, emails go through Ethereal (a disposable test mailbox) and are never delivered to a real inbox. Email is used only for account recovery.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and chat data for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual chats at any time from the sidebar. To remove all of your data, delete your account or remove the local tinygpt.db file.

7. Security

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (10 rounds); plaintext passwords are never stored;
  • Reset tokens are 256-bit random values, stored only as SHA-256 hashes, and expire after 1 hour;
  • Sessions use httpOnly cookies and are regenerated at login;
  • API endpoints reject unauthenticated requests.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be raised with the operator of this local deployment.
